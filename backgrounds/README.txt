Put your own background pictures in this folder (.jpg, .png, .webp, .bmp).

They show up in the Live preview's "Background" dropdown (click "Refresh" after adding files).
Any size works: pictures are scaled and centre-cropped to fill the frame. 16:9 images such as
1920x1080 or 1280x720 fit best. For a realistic look, use a photo of a room that is slightly
out of focus, as a real room behind a streamer would be.
